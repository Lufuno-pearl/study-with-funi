Get Funi on your phone

  1. iPhone: open this page in Safari. Android: use Chrome.
  2. Tap Share (square with an arrow), or the ⋮ menu on Android.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Funi from your home screen, then turn on notifications (bell › switch at the top).
